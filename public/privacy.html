Privacy

MvL OS, the web desktop at mennovanleeuwen.nl, run by Menno van Leeuwen.

Without signing in

Nothing about you is kept on the server. Notes, layouts and settings you make as a guest stay in your browser's storage.

When you sign in with GitHub

The server keeps, in one database:

  • Your GitHub account id, login, name and avatar address, and when you were last seen.
  • GitHub's access token for this app (encrypted). Sign-in only asks for your public profile; private repositories are only reached if you allow them in Settings → Code hosts.
  • Your sign-in sessions (30 days each).
  • What syncs between your devices: notes, window layouts, desktop icons, launchers, dock and settings.
  • Agents threads, their attachments, and what the agent remembers for you.
  • Services you link yourself, with their tokens or passwords encrypted: Gitea/Forgejo, Google Calendar, CalDAV, Seafile, OnlyOffice, updown.io, Ollama Cloud and web search providers.
  • Push subscriptions for the devices you turn notifications on for.

Files you make without Seafile linked are stored in your browser only, never on this server, and are gone when you clear the site's data.

Who sees it

Only you. Other users have no way to see anything of yours. The admin sees a list of accounts with their login, avatar, when they joined and were last seen, which kinds of services are linked, and how much storage they use; never the contents of notes, threads, memories or files.

When you use a linked service, the server talks to it on your behalf: GitHub, Google, your own Gitea, CalDAV or Seafile server, and Ollama Cloud (the Agents app sends your messages there to get answers). Their own privacy terms apply to what they receive.

Deleting it

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything listed above from the server at once, signs you out everywhere, revokes Google and Seafile access, and removes this app from your GitHub account. Files in your own Seafile are not touched. Signing out alone clears what this browser kept for you.
